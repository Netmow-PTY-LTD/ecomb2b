Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about B2B ordering, MOQ, and storage.

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) for wholesale delivery?

Our standard minimum wholesale order value is RM 250 across mixed products. Free refrigerated cold-chain delivery is unlocked for all orders above RM 800 in Klang Valley, Penang, and Johor Bahru. Individual items also have master-pack MOQs (usually 3 to 10 packs) as noted on each listing.

How long do the Ready-to-Eat retort packs last?

Our retort meals have a shelf life of 12 to 18 months. Retort pouch items can be stored at ambient temperature before opening without cold-chain dependence. Frozen items (such as smoked meats and marinated skewers) maintain peak freshness for 9 to 12 months at -18°C.

How do we apply for Net 30 Commercial Credit Terms?

Registered B2B buyers with valid SSM registration can request 30 or 45-day credit lines. Once verified by our finance team, credit terms are activated directly in your Buyer Portal profile.

Can you OEM retort pack our restaurant's secret recipes?

Yes! We provide private label manufacturing and recipe scaling under strict confidentiality (NDA). Visit our Wholesale & OEM Page or submit an enquiry to schedule sample pilot trials.
