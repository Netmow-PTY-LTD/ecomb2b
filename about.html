Our Story & Purpose

Established in 2021 in Subang Jaya, Selangor, DapurWarisan RTE Wholesale (M) Sdn Bhd was born from a simple realization: modern foodservice operators struggle with soaring kitchen labour costs, chef turnover, culinary inconsistency, and volatile raw ingredient wastage.

By developing industrial-grade retort sterilization and vacuum sous-vide packaging for complex Malaysian heritage dishes — like 8-hour Rendang Tok and smoked meats — we enable hotels, cafes, and catering operators to deliver five-star dining in under five minutes with predictable portion margins.

Our Mission

To empower Malaysian F&B businesses with zero-waste, portion-controlled, authentic food products of uncompromising quality.

Our Vision

To become Southeast Asia's benchmark B2B RTE food supply and retort contract manufacturing partner.

Engineering Food Safety

Our Technical Capabilities

State-of-the-art thermal processing and clean-room packaging infrastructure.

Steam-Air Retort Autoclaves

Computerized counter-pressure autoclaves subject sealed pouches to 121.1°C commercial sterilization. This destroys vegetative pathogens and bacterial spores while maintaining tender meat texture and locking in natural juices without chemical preservatives.

-38°C Cryogenic Blast Chillers

Freshly prepared smoked ribs, briskets, and satay skewers pass through high-velocity blast freezers within 20 minutes of cooking. Rapid micro-crystallization prevents cellular damage, ensuring that upon re-heating, meat yields zero purge and maximum juiciness.

4-Layer Barrier Packaging

Our packaging utilizes high-barrier PET / Aluminium / BOPA / Cast Polypropylene films with zero pin-hole risk. Provides complete barrier resistance against moisture, oxygen, UV degradation, and punctures during pallet transport.

Audited Cold-Chain Logistics

Dedicated fleet of multi-temperature refrigerated trucks equipped with ThermoKing chillers and real-time GPS temperature data loggers. Maintain uninterrupted -18°C integrity from factory loading dock to client receiving bays.
